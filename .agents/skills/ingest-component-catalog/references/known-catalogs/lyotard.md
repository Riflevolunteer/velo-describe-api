## Lyotard pedal catalogue, German / English export edition (4 pp, c. 1977-81; forum.tontonvelo.com t=605) — data_source 380

- Source: Tonton Vélo "LYOTARD, 1977 catalogue" thread (t=605), attachment
  `download/file.php?id=143859` ("LYOTARD 1977.pdf", 4 pp scan, no text
  layer). Label "Lyotard pedal catalogue, German / English edition (c. 1977-81)".
- Dating: the poster says 1977. Printer mark "IQ (77) 30.06.15", where
  (77) is probably the Loire phone prefix, not a year. The 460 D BMX range
  (black / red / gold / blue anodised) argues for c. 1980-81. A 1981 French
  magazine advert (t=6468, file id 38687) repeats the 460 D (91 mm, 324 g),
  23 piste, 79 (88 mm, 280 g) and 65 / 65 CN (90 mm, 367 g) text word for word.
- Contents: REF. 77 R (men, highway), 136 R (race), 5 R (youngster), 25 R
  (townsman), 99 R (ladies), 25 A, 460 A (steel, 90 mm), 36 / 36 RCM (steel,
  90 mm, RCM with reflector), 3 R / 3 RB / 3 RN, 66 R / RB / RN, 88 R, 75 R
  (one-piece reflecting block), 82 (alloy monobloc, 340 g/pair), 65 / 65 N,
  23 (track, steel hub and plate), 45 CA (steel hub, 88 mm, 309 g) / 45 CAN
  (black), 79, PL 200 (alloy, 280 g), 460 D, 460 D BMX, 77 RN (BMX steel).
  All 9/16" and 14 x 1.25 DIN. Page 4 also has steel rims (no models) and
  an exploded view of the 3 R.
- Added: 10979 "Lyotard 77R" (Pedals, brand 417, 1981-1983; year_from is
  the latest plausible catalogue date, an upper bound; year_to from the
  1982-83 Peugeot specs). Linked bike_spec 11279 (1982 Peugeot P 10
  "Lyotard 77 R"), 11599 (1982 Peugeot NL H 10), 11728 (1983 Peugeot UO 5
  Club); year-ranged overrides added in generate-bike-update-sql.js.
- Checked, no change: 3801 136R (1970-89), 3808 36 (1950-87), 3811 45CA
  (1930-83), 3813 460A (1975-84), 3815 460D (1970-81), 3818 65 (1970-80),
  3819 79 (1970-84), 3820 82 (1970-87) all cover the catalogue.
- Not done: no rows for 25 R, 25 A, 3 R, 66 R, 75 R, 88 R, 77 RN, 45 CAN,
  65 N, PL 200 or the 460 D BMX colours (no bike specs name them). Rows
  3817 5R, 3822 99 and 3800 MB 23TF have no years; this catalogue could
  date them c. 1977-81. PL 200 may or may not be 3823 "LA PL 2000" (1980).
  Lyotard 90 (spec 12407, 1984) is not in this catalogue.
