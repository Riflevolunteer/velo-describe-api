## Brooks "Saddle-Comfort" catalogue — c. 1938 (bulgier.net `1937_cy.pdf`, data_source 284)

**1938 scan availability, 2026-11-24:** The [Ebykr List 265 second-edition scan](https://ebykr.com/library/brooks-1938-catalog-saddle-comfort-list-no-265-2nd-edition/) identifies its copy as clearer but states that the remaining pages are held in its collection and are not reproduced; the published selection omits the spring-seat table and model pages. It therefore cannot settle the two 1938 height fractions. No database changes made; a scan of printed pages 27–29 remains needed.

**1937 spring-seat source comparison, 2026-11-24:** The maker's [1937 catalogue, printed p.13](https://ebykr.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ebykr-1937-brooks-catalog-plyflex-p209-and-suplex-s25-pp12-13-2095px.webp), lists S25/NH as 10¾ × 6¾ × 2¾ inches, 1 lb 13 oz; [p.24](https://ebykr.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ebykr-1937-brooks-catalog-suplex-s20-and-b66-champion-pp24-25-2095px.webp) lists S20/2 as 11 × 11 × 3¾ inches, 3 lb 14 oz. The same models and construction appear in the 1938 scan, but their listed weights differ (2 lb and 3 lb 12 oz). These earlier dimensions support checking the disputed 1938 fractions but do not independently establish unchanged 1938 dimensions. The 1938 source remains too blurred for a confident height reading; no database change was made.

**Remaining leather-table check, 2026-11-24:** Compared the remaining 1938-derived dimensions of B31 5274, Flyweight Narrow 9794, B11 9795, B32 9796, B90/1 9807, B35 9808 and B80 9811 with the printed [1938 catalogue](http://bulgier.net/pics/bike/Catalogs/Brooks/1937_cy.pdf), pages 4, 6, 9, 13, 20–23. Other descriptions spanning editions use later specifications: for example B17 Flyer 5308 agrees with the [1957 catalogue](http://bulgier.net/pics/bike/Catalogs/Brooks/1957_cs.pdf), PDF page 3, rather than the 1938 dimensions. A difference between editions alone is not a transcription error. The individual leather values corrected in earlier checks are recorded below. The two spring-seat height fractions remain unresolved because the 1938 scan is blurred; the [1933 catalogue](https://ebykr.com/library/brooks-1933-catalog-saddles-and-riding-kit-for-cyclists-and-motor-cyclists-list-no-168/) lists S20/2 as 11 × 11 × 3¾ inches but does not independently verify the 1938 dimension.
