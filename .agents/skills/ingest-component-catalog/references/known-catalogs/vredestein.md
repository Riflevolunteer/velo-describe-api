## R. J. Chicken & Sons trade catalogue, Vredestein section — 7 November 1979 (Tyres_from_R_J_Chicken_catalogue_1979-11-07.pdf, 6 pp, scan, no text layer), data_source id 179

- Source: https://www.icenicam.org.uk/library/Vredestein/Tyres_from_R_J_Chicken_catalogue_1979-11-07.pdf
  (IceniCAM, found by browsing the folder listing; each company folder holds
  its own extract). The Vredestein section (cat. pp. 41-42) of the UK
  distributor R. J. Chicken & Sons Ltd's trade catalogue; the same 7 Nov 1979
  catalogue as Maillard (data_source 104), Selle Italia (176) and Sedis (177).
  PDF pp.: 1 catalogue index, 2 blank, 3 = cat. p. 41 (Slimline Sprint), 4 =
  cat. p. 42 (quote, Butilux tube, Vredestein / Weinmann rim), 5 = the pink
  trade price sheet (cat. p. 68, Vredestein covers and tubes bottom left),
  6 IceniCAM cover.
- Dated by filename (7 Nov 1979); a UK distributor listing is a first-
  appearance record, not a start date. The 700C size is flagged "NEW".
- Only one tyre: the Slimline Sprint, "the new concept high quality sports
  tyre", 27 x 1-1/4 x 1-1/16 (25-630), an ultra-lightweight narrow-section
  tyre for 14mm rims, ribbed racing tread, transparent gumwall sides, a body
  of long-fibre Egyptian cotton, 13oz, "no wider than a new penny", quoted
  as "thoroughly recommended for fast touring ... Cyclist Touring Club".
  Also sold as 28 x 1-5/8 x 1-1/16 (700C). Trade prices 2.55 per tyre in
  packs of 25 (both sizes); Butilux tubes 1.00 in packs of 20.
- Existing Vredestein rows before this pass (brand 660, both bare velobase,
  untouched): 6905 Fortezza TriComp (1980-90), 6906 Trimmer Cross (1970).
- Inserted (1979-1979, UUID source_id, source_ref 179, group_id NULL): 8771
  "Vredestein Slimline Sprint (27 x 1-1/4 x 1-1/16)" and 8772 "Vredestein
  Slimline Sprint (700C)", two rows because the sizes are different tyres
  (630mm and 622mm bead seat); search_text for both is "Vredestein Slimline
  Sprint".
- No existing rows updated, deleted or merged; no bike_spec links changed.
- Does not help link the kept Raleigh bikes: five specs name "Vredestein
  700 x 20C Racer High Pressure" (also "Racer HP"), which is not in this
  catalogue, so they stay unlinked until a source for that tyre turns up.
- Out of scope: Butilux inner tubes (presta, about 4oz; no Tubes category),
  the special thick rubber rim tape ("NEW"), the Vredestein / Weinmann rim
  photo (a joint-development caption with no model; see the Weinmann
  section of the same catalogue).
