## Catalogue Provisoire, January 1946 — applied and validated 2026-11-24

Source 555; [catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/super_champion_catalogue_provisoire_-_janvier_1946.html). Added Sport Leger Tandem 12528 and Nivex steel and light-alloy rear hubs 12529/12530. Enriched 4809 Sport 46 Dural and 4810 Sport Leger; old descriptions and years are recorded in the [source comparison](../source-hunts.md#super-champion-1936-and-1946-catalogue-review--2026-11-24). Corrected 4809 from 1940–1940 to 1946–1946 because the catalogue calls it new; extended 4810 from 1940–1940 to 1940–1946. No merges or deletions; 4798/4809 require further distinguishing evidence. No prior maker catalogue in this sequence establishes a complete list of products dropped between editions.

For both loads, readback verified each changed row and a repeat application affected zero rows. The post-load brand near-duplicate report returned no pairs; no changed or added component has bike-spec links.
