# BSA component sources

## BSA bicycle replacement parts list — November 1934, reconciled 2026-11-25

The [41-page list](https://www.icenicam.org.uk/library/BSA/Bicycles_parts_list_1934-11-01.pdf) states on printed p. 1 that it supplies replacement parts for BSA bicycles in the 1933 and later ranges. Its cover dates prices to 1 November 1934; printed p. 36 says October 1934. Source **565** records the list. Part numbers in the BSA tables are replacement-order numbers, not necessarily a physical manufacturer's model code. The dates on added records mean the list attests the replacement offering in 1934, not known production start/end.

Applied and read back **12582–12596**: BSA New Pattern Roller chains 5435/5437 (12582/12583, printed p. 30), BSA front 6025 and rear 6027 caliper brakes (12584/12585, p. 12), and named saddles (p. 13): Terry 1086, 1199G, 1198G, 1199L, 1087, M1042G, M1042L, 1088G and 1088L (12586–12594); Mansfield Ormond (12595); Dunlop F26C (12596). The printed BSA order 4282 is shared by the two Terry Trichrome listings; it is not asserted as Terry's model code. The list does not establish further construction details for most saddles. Created Terry brand and the BSA Chains/Brakes, Terry Saddles and Dunlop Saddles category associations. No existing component rows were changed or removed.

The guarded SQL rerun changed zero rows; BSA and Terry near-duplicate checks returned zero pairs, and the database quality report returned zero structural errors. Generator/live comparison covered 118 catalogues and 24,829 specs, with zero gains or changed mappings and the same 15 pre-existing Huret Rival losses as baseline. No bicycle links were changed.

**Hub and control comparison, 2026-11-25:** Printed p. 17 lists six complete, separately ordered BSA front hubs. The only existing BSA front hub **3218** is the Hublite generator hub; **3216** is a coaster, and the [3217 Continental De Luxe photograph](https://www.velobase.com/CompImages/Hubs/D537267A-0550-48D4-9287-31D57EA00620.jpeg) shows a high-flange rear hub. These do not identify the six ordinary fronts. The front models differ by pattern, spindle, use, drilling or lubrication; 4257's carrier spindle and standard 5480 can use the same shell, and B/E fronts also share a listed shell, so assembly differences rather than an assumption of six unrelated hub-shell designs justify comparing them separately. Applied 5480, 4260, 4250, 4257, 4255 and 4269 as components **12597–12602**, with BSA replacement-order numbers explicitly identified as such. Readback verified all six; SQL rerun changed zero rows, BSA near-duplicate review found zero pairs, quality report found zero errors, and bike-link comparison showed no gains or changes with the 15 pre-existing Huret Rival losses unchanged. The pictured 3217 rear cannot be crosswalked to any of the four listed rear orders. The 4266 complete rear listing says 40 holes while the lightweight replacement shell on p. 19 says 32; do not silently resolve that conflict.

Printed p. 21 illustrates BSA handlebar control 27Z and top-tube control 10Y. The VeloBase references for existing round-framed **5954** and top-tube **5955** have no published photographs; their generic titles do not uniquely crosswalk either assembly. Do not add or retitle a control solely on the family resemblance. Four rear-hub orders and other inventoried categories have the identity limits recorded below.

**Remaining category review, 2026-11-25:** Printed pp. 6–10 list handlebar and stem assemblies by bicycle fitment. Several replacement numbers recur across different configurations, and the page does not establish who made each bar; retain their information as fitment evidence without creating coded standalone bars. Pp. 14–17 list replacement wheels by bicycle and include tyres/parts exclusions; an order number does not establish a uniquely branded wheel model. Pp. 17–19 offer four rear-hub orders 4252, 4256, 4266 and 5394, but the high-flange **3217** specimen lacks a model crosswalk and the catalogue does not illustrate each listed assembly separately. The 4266 hole-count conflict above remains unresolved. Pp. 16 and 20 illustrate a BSA three-speed hub, but a unique generation crosswalk to generic **2692** is not established. Pp. 23–24 name an Eadie coaster and Sturmey-Archer, Trivelox and derailleur hub-brake fitments; BSA order numbers do not identify the physical manufacturer's hub model or prove that the named gear system made the hub. Avoid adding duplicate or wrongly attributed hubs.

Pp. 26–29 show hub sprocket fitments and BSA-pattern freewheels, but the available order tables do not crosswalk the freewheels to **2054/2055**, or establish the physical maker of each sprocket for mixed hub types. P. 32 distinguishes A21 flanged Standard and flangeless Supereze detachable chainwheels; the photo on p. 35 does not identify either against **1228/1229**. The numbered Popular wheel-crank items are individual right-side assemblies rather than complete paired cranksets. The pictured new-pattern rat-trap pedal has no code assigned, and the existing 3682/3683 specimen comparison remains as in the [source hunt](../source-hunts.md#bsa-rat-trap-pedal-comparison--2026-11-23).

P. 35 additionally offers three widths of rubber roadster pedals under the BSA pedal heading; those are distinct in construction from existing BSA rat-trap pedals **3682–3684**. P. 12 lists 6023 as a complete hand lever with clip under BSA caliper brakes, also listed for tandem hub-brake controls on p. 27. Applied pedal-family component **12603**, source UUID `0140e843-17ae-4dc5-a049-de4e7a0d7f6c`, and lever **12604**, source UUID `a11e8d60-9d6c-4d7c-8860-eb0e95e9c235`, as BSA replacement offerings; their physical manufacturer markings are not established by this parts list. Added the BSA Brake Levers category association. Readback confirmed both; rerun changed zero rows, BSA near-duplicate review found zero pairs, and the quality report found zero errors. Generator/live comparison remained unchanged with the same 15 pre-existing Huret Rival losses; none of the 23 additions from source 565 has a bike-spec link. Loose levers, axles, bearings, frame hardware, accessories and other service components are outside complete-component scope. Pp. 2–5 cover frames/forks; pp. 33–36 are repair charges, a gear chart, pedal page and conditions. The loose service-part listings and the catalogue's partial bicycle fitment scope do not establish production endpoints.
