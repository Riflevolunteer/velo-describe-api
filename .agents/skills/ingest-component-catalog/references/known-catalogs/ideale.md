## c.1936 maker catalogue — source assessment 2026-11-25

The [Tron & Berthet Idéale catalogue](https://ebykr.com/library/ideale-c-1936-catalog-tron-berthet-title-page-and-pages-3-to-11/) comprises the title page and printed pp.3–11, with accessible scans and a transcription. Ebykr estimates c.1936; no year is printed in the pages, so the estimate is not a firm production date. Pages 3–9 cover road, racing, touring and ladies' models alongside out-of-scope children's and motorised saddles; pp.10–11 list rubber-topped Idéale-Dunlop and motorised models. The catalogue uses the same numbers for different tops and leather grades, and names the Elaedy and Nortex grades separately: do not equate them or date an individual specimen from its number alone.

At triage, a live search found 58 brand-580 VeloBase-source records with descriptions at most 20 characters longer than their titles, 47 with a missing date bound. These are possible review targets, not a count of matches: inspect their markings, construction and variants against the scans. The catalogue's pp.5–7 distinguish standard and lightweight 63/64 versions, while pp.6 and 11 show different No.41 top materials. Existing Ron Kitching source 280 and the 1955 maker advert source 546 concern other evidence; source-title and citation searches found no registration of this c.1936 edition. A brand-scoped near-duplicate report found no pairs; this does not exclude differently named or uncoded overlaps.
