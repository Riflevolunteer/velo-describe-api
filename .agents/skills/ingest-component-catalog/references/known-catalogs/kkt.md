## RT-E1 source follow-up — 2026-11-25 (read-only)

Visually inspected page 9 of the [original 1979 Miyata catalogue scan](https://www.ragandbone.ca/PDFs/Miyata%20Catalogue%20PDFs/1979%20MIyata%20Catalogue.pdf): the table headed `'79 Model Specification` prints **KKT RT-E1** for both Model 310 and Model 200. This confirms the printed fitment spelling in that edition, not the separate 1980 OCR-normalized values, nor a crosswalk to 1974 RT-E 3773 or the later RT-E II. No construction or maker-code legend accompanies the cells. A modern [seller listing labelled RT-E II](https://screaminwhs.base.shop/items/135353818) is a specimen lead, but its title alone is not a dated manufacturer key or an E1/E comparison. Searches for KKT/ Kyokuto code variants and maker material did not recover a qualifying crosswalk. Keep the existing hold and seek a maker key or coded comparative originals. No database or link changes.


## Panasonic identity review — 2026-11-18 (read-only)

Panasonic 1985 printed p.12 explicitly distinguishes **RT-E** (Sport 1000) from **RT-E II** (Sport LX); p.13 also names RT-E II. Neither is an explicit maker bridge for Miyata RT-E1. Keep the ten 1979–82 specs NULL and 1974 RT-E **3773** unchanged. The 1980 Miyata chart is an OCR re-render with context-normalized spellings, which Panasonic cannot independently validate.
