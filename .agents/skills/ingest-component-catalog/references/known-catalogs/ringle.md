- **Bubba's revision is explicit, not inferred from weight alone.** Maker
  [2353](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/img-2353-1.jpg)
  calls the1995 front Bubba updated with aero dust caps and lists127g.
  Current12054 retains Branford's115g/traditional-flange description.
  The source supplies a concrete construction distinction; inspect the
  retailer illustration/edition before overwriting115g, aggregating versions
  or inserting a duplicate. Its28/32H/100mm/12mm axle details are explicit.
- **SuperEight's alloy claim differs by source.** The same maker page says
  **7075-T6**, while current12053 deliberately preserves Branford's printed
  **7076-T6**. The maker is stronger evidence for the component material;
  retain the retailer's literal reading in provenance notes rather than
  silently treating it as the manufacturer's statement. Maker also confirms
  130/135mm road/mountain spacing, four6001 bearings and28H as well as32/36H.
- **Zooka road is distinct from mountain fitment.** Maker
  [2354](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/img-2354-1.jpg)
  specifies26.0mm road versus25.4mm mountain bars. Road110/120mm,
  -12.5deg/0deg offerings are separately tabulated; do not automatically
  add them to12048's mountain-oriented description or create a new row
  without first clarifying that existing broadly titled row's identity.
- **Weight bases explain why generic numbers can mislead.** Quill mountain
  245/255/261/292g map to105/120/135/150mm with1in expander; larger expanders
  add10/20g. [Clamp-On2355](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/img-2355-1.jpg)
  gives180g specifically at135mm/0deg/1in; current12049's180mm length is a
  retailer configuration, not a typo to remove because this page stops at150mm.
- **Moby's220g is also configuration-specific:** maker
  [2357](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/img-2357-2.jpg)
  gives26.8x350mm, Easton EA70-T6 tube,7075-T6 clamp parts and one-bolt head.
  Do not merge12045 into later Sun Moby Deuce10173 (215g) on tube material,
  length or the Moby name. Existing cross-catalogue brand/version decision stands.
- SuperBubba [2352](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/img-2352-1.jpg)
  explicitly specifies16mm axle,19mm clamping surface, two6001 bearings,
