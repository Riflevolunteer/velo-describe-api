# Cyclo Gear Company catalogues

British Cyclo of Birmingham is brand **193**. The French Cyclo range has its own [catalogue notes](le-cyclo.md) under brand **192**.

## Oppy and Super Olympic — comparison, 2026-11-23

The [1949 catalogue, scan 3](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_-_1949_scan_3.html) depicts a three-speed Oppy with integral chain tensioning, down-tube control, 13–15T top and up to 22T low recommended freewheel, and a braze-on bracket or optional clip. The shape and twin cable attachment match [4209's specimen](https://www.velobase.com/Pages/ViewComponent.aspx?ID=7531A88B-E2C1-48FA-83B5-74FD27E88A15). The catalogue attests 1949 availability, not introduction or discontinuation. Applied description enrichment retaining 1938–1949; old description was `Cyclo Gear Company Cyclo Oppy, England`, source 1; new source is 532. Readback confirmed the 190-character description and source, and a second SQL run affected zero rows. Generator/live comparison across 118 catalogues and 24,829 specs returned the same 15 pre-existing Huret discrepancies, with no gained or changed mappings.

The [1949 catalogue, scan 2](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_-_1949_scan_2.html) describes a three- or four-speed Super Olympic Professional racing assembly, 600 g complete, with close-ratio freewheel options through 21T. [4213](https://www.velobase.com/Pages/ViewComponent.aspx?ID=9406F010-E95E-4CE8-9515-C50DB679A544) and [4214](https://www.velobase.com/Pages/ViewComponent.aspx?ID=B79AB8D7-2835-4A79-B380-0A5430794BDA) show similar Olympic-marked mechanisms, but the available views do not establish whether 4213 represents the earlier 1947 cage or the illustrated 1949 variant, or whether either assembly duplicates the other. Compare dated maker drawings or coded complete specimens before changing dates or merging. Live 4213 is 1947–1947 and 4214 is 1948–1950, both source 1. None of 4209, 4213 or 4214 has bike links or generator ID overrides; the Cyclo near-duplicate report returned no pairs, which does not settle these specimens.

## Benelux catalogue 628/G/S — preliminary comparison

The [32-page catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_benelux_catalogue_628gs.html) is attributed by the archive to August 1962. Pages 2–11 repeat the Super 60, Mark 7, Mark 8 Tourist, controls and front changers in No. 609, but p.2 adds a SUSIX six-speed package and BEFOX six-speed freewheel. It explicitly says the five-speed mechanism is unsuitable for six-speed use. Page 7 again lists L19/25 (6012). Page 12 lists Standard brackets and clips, not a complete Standard gear; pages 13–19 and 21 largely repeat No. 609's chainwheels, chain, freewheels and sprockets. Page 20 lists BEN/2/SA for additional Sturmey-Archer hub types and BEN/3/SA and BEN/9/SA two- and three-sprocket blocks. Page 21 gives SOLO 18–26 or 28 teeth, while No. 609 lists 16–26 or 28. Pages 22–30 are chiefly accessories and spares; pages 31–32 repeat the Rosa cranks and hubs. These are catalogue attestations, not production date bounds. The hub conversion blocks are reconciled below; SHAN and 20/26 remain ambiguous.

The 2026-11-23 page review also confirms 1962 listings for existing 12378–12384 (pp.13, 18–19) and 12386–12395 (pp.21, 31–32). Applied year_to 1960 → 1962 and source 523 → 527 to those 17 rows; all other fields remain unchanged, including the 1960 SOLO range valid for that earlier edition. None has bike links. Readback verified all rows and a second SQL run affected zero rows. The Cyclo near-duplicate report returned no pairs; generator/live comparison of 118 catalogues and 24,829 specs reproduced the same 15 previously recorded Huret discrepancies, with no gains or changed mappings. Page 23 lists FS/32 racing sprockets, but its relationship to SPREE 12387 is not established. Pages 15–17 and 22–30 otherwise concern service parts, accessories and fittings outside the component categories.

Applied as source **527**: six-speed Super 60 rear **12409** and BEFOX freewheel **12410**, both attested in 1962; L19/25 **6012** year_to 1960 → 1962, source 523 → 527, with all other fields retained. ### Hub conversion blocks — applied and validated 2026-11-23

[628/G/S p.20](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_benelux_catalogue_628gs_-_page_20.html) lists BEN/2/SA as the top-tube conversion gear sold **without sprockets**, for several three- and four-speed hubs. BEN/3/SA is a three-spline two-sprocket block (15–19T top, 17–26T low); BEN/9/SA is the three-sprocket block (15–16T, 17–20T, 18–24T), with a recessed low sprocket and a hub spindle modification. The latter provides nine or twelve ratios. The page is attributed to 1962, not evidence of production start or end.

Live identity searches found the existing BEN/2/SA assembly 12385 (1960–1960, source 523), but no identified separate blocks. Its description associates the two-sprocket sizes with the gear and limits it to AW/SW and six ratios; the later page supports broadening that description and extending the attested end to 1962. Applied source 527: 12385 year_to 1960 → 1962, source 523 → 527, and description updated to reflect the separately sold blocks and broader hub compatibility. Its previous description was “Top-tube-controlled rear gear for Sturmey-Archer AW or SW, providing six ratios. 1/8 in Benelux sprockets, 15–19T top and 17–26T low. Adapter replaces the hub dust cap. The 3-spline two-sprocket block was BEN/3/SA.” Added cassette-category fixed sprocket blocks BEN/3/SA **12414** and BEN/9/SA **12415**, each attested in 1962. These blocks are not freewheels. Readback confirmed fields and description lengths; SQL rerun affected zero rows. The brand duplicate report returned no groups. No loaded bike spec mentions Benelux or these codes, and 12385 has no bike links. ### Catalogue and specimen distinctions

- **Freewheels, pp.15–19.** The existing two-speed 2090 has the BLUSH/2 face markings, two recessed ring holes and two notches. Its outer sprocket has 21 pointed teeth. The catalogue lists BLUSH/2 with 16T top and 20T low, so the link between its exact as-sold configuration and 2090 remains open. BEFO is the listed five-speed B.2 in 3/32 in. Five-speed specimen 2092 has square-form teeth and a compatible-looking Type B face. The p.16 service illustrations also show Type B markings, so the name alone does not identify its body as BEFO. 2091 carries distinct Benelux Star branding. Hold BLUSH/2 and BEFO inserts pending exact crosswalks. [Freewheel drawings](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_18.html)
- **Other controls and gears, pp.10–12.** SHAN is the rod-controlled Mark 7 front chain shifter with a conical grip, not a rear gear control. The [1961 Holdsworth listing](https://ebykr.com/library/holdsworth-1961-aids-catalog/) separately describes SHAN as a seat-tube front changer; catalogue 609 p.11 names its cage, operating rod and clip parts and recommends up to 20 teeth difference. These sources confirm the family function but do not distinguish the three specimen variants. Later rod variants 2329–2331 have distinguishing specimen features; the catalogue drawing does not identify one uniquely. The cable specimen 2327 has a raised Benelux cage and differs from the SHIF drawing. The separate lower p.11 frame-control assembly is Ref. 20/26. The bare Cyclo 6018 and Birmingham-marked 6011 are leads. 6011 is currently assigned to French brand 192 and needs a specimen or parts-sheet match to identify the model. P.12 offers the 3/4-speed Standard alongside bracket variants; it does not date the specific C-hanger 4210 or triangle-hanger 4211. [Control diagrams](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_11.html)
- **Rings and chain, pp.13–14.** The specimen in 1253 is a bare five-pin double stamped 51/46. P.13's Rosa stock list lacks 51T. French Rosa ring 1252 has the catalogue drawing's branched spoke form and five fixing points. It remains a lead for the separately listed ring. P.14 advertises a Brampton-Benelux combination with 1/2 × 3/32 in, 114 links and 13 oz, without a Brampton model number. The existing Brampton chain records have no positively identified match. [Chainwheel options](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_13.html) · [Chain advertisement](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_14.html)
- Pp.15–17 principally cover the complete freewheels' service parts and display fixtures. Pp.22–30 cover carded accessories, fittings, cable, hub spares and brake blocks. Pp.33–44 cover tools and workshop repairs. The Mark 12 name on p.40 identifies spanner fitment. P.44 identifies FR/2 jig compatibility for the new 21-tooth ratchet and three-pawl Benelux construction.
