# Cyclo Gear Company catalogues

British Cyclo of Birmingham is brand **193**. The French Cyclo range has its own [catalogue notes](le-cyclo.md) under brand **192**.

## Catalogue No. 609, August 1960 — applied and validated 2026-11-23

[No. 609](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609.html) contains 44 numbered pages. P.1 prints August 1960. The [archive's catalogue sequence](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_gear_company_-_catalogues_etc.html) gives August/September. The imprint dates the offerings. Production starts and ends require independent evidence. The comparison here covers No. 609. The earlier catalogues and 628/G/S can establish which products were introduced or carried over.

### Catalogue and specimen distinctions

- **Freewheels, pp.15–19.** The existing two-speed 2090 has the BLUSH/2 face markings, two recessed ring holes and two notches. Its outer sprocket has 21 pointed teeth. The catalogue lists BLUSH/2 with 16T top and 20T low, so the link between its exact as-sold configuration and 2090 remains open. BEFO is the listed five-speed B.2 in 3/32 in. Five-speed specimen 2092 has square-form teeth and a compatible-looking Type B face. The p.16 service illustrations also show Type B markings, so the name alone does not identify its body as BEFO. 2091 carries distinct Benelux Star branding. Hold BLUSH/2 and BEFO inserts pending exact crosswalks. [Freewheel drawings](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_18.html)
- **Other controls and gears, pp.10–12.** SHAN is the direct Mark 7 control with a conical grip. Later rod variants 2329–2331 have distinguishing specimen features; the catalogue drawing does not identify one uniquely. The cable specimen 2327 has a raised Benelux cage and differs from the SHIF drawing. The separate lower p.11 frame-control assembly is Ref. 20/26. The bare Cyclo 6018 and Birmingham-marked 6011 are leads. 6011 is currently assigned to French brand 192 and needs a specimen or parts-sheet match to identify the model. P.12 offers the 3/4-speed Standard alongside bracket variants; it does not date the specific C-hanger 4210 or triangle-hanger 4211. [Control diagrams](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_11.html)
- **Rings and chain, pp.13–14.** The specimen in 1253 is a bare five-pin double stamped 51/46. P.13's Rosa stock list lacks 51T. French Rosa ring 1252 has the catalogue drawing's branched spoke form and five fixing points. It remains a lead for the separately listed ring. P.14 advertises a Brampton-Benelux combination with 1/2 × 3/32 in, 114 links and 13 oz, without a Brampton model number. The existing Brampton chain records have no positively identified match. [Chainwheel options](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_13.html) · [Chain advertisement](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/cyclo_catalogue_609_-_page_14.html)
- Pp.15–17 principally cover the complete freewheels' service parts and display fixtures. Pp.22–30 cover carded accessories, fittings, cable, hub spares and brake blocks. Pp.33–44 cover tools and workshop repairs. The Mark 12 name on p.40 identifies spanner fitment. P.44 identifies FR/2 jig compatibility for the new 21-tooth ratchet and three-pawl Benelux construction.
