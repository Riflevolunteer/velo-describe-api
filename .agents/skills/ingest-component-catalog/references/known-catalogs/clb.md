The CC4's curved blade and hood and the RS2's looped hood and long blade agree with the named models in [January 1957 catalogue](https://forum.tontonvelo.com/viewtopic.php?t=50662) printed pp.4–5; the 1957 source supplies 23.5 mm clamp and cylindrical cable end for CC4 and the RS1 raw / RS2 polished finish pairing. The attributed c.1954 catalogue p.7 also names CC4 and RS1/RS2. The 1951 source attests CC4 and RS2 availability, not a proven introduction year or separate 1951 RS1 availability.

Live **12399** and **12400** each have 1957–1957 and source 524. Both have zero bike links; no CC4/RS2 specs or generator ID overrides found. Applied `year_from` 1957 → 1951 on both and registered the advertisement as source 531 (source 524 → 531); descriptions and 1957 ends remain unchanged. The combined RS1/RS2 row represents the family, not a claim that RS1 is separately attested in 1951. The prior values above permit restoration. CLB near-duplicate report found zero pairs. Before changes, generator/live comparison covered 118 catalogues and 24,829 specs: the same 15 known Huret discrepancies, no gained or changed mappings. Readback confirmed both rows and source 531; SQL rerun affected zero rows.
