## 1994 Components Catalog and price lists — applied and validated 2026-11-23

Source 519: [manufacturer catalogue photographs and accompanying sheets](https://www.gringineer.com/blogs/default-blog/1994-wilderness-trail-bikes-catalog-and-price-list). The cover prints 1994; two WTB price sheets separately print 1994. The threaded bottom bracket page advertises early 1994 availability. A separate Specialized tyre sheet is undated and a separate letter is dated 15 June 1993; these were not used to date one another. The American Classic 31.8 × 425 mm seatpost on the price list was not assigned to an existing model without a crosswalk. Frames, pads, tools and accessories are outside component scope.
