### What the evidence settles

**Road-stem weights are an internal maker-source conflict.** Illustrated
[0013](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/titec1997_0013.jpg)
prints185g quill /115g Ahead, exactly as Guida1999 and current11830/11831.
The same year's dealer sheet prints175g/145g. No explicit weighing basis,
length-specific crosswalk or revision statement resolves it. Retain both
maker claims; do not replace the stored185g/115g with dealer weights, invent
a generation split, or create duplicate road titanium stems. The catalogue
adds6Al-4V bosses, aero clamps, Ahead's internal T-Slot binder and the wider
90–130mm /0 or-17deg offering.

**Al-235 material differs by source, not by transcription.** Maker
[0010](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0661/0501/6425/files/titec1997_0010.jpg)
and dealer sheet agree on2014 alloy,350mm and235g. Guida1999 really prints
7075-T6/350mm/240g under Al235, while Guida1996 really gives270mm/200–235g.
Retain those edition-specific claims in11612; no demonstrated production
transition or basis for deleting the later specification.

**C191 is explicitly distinct from Carbon Road in this edition.** Maker0010
gives350mm/191g roll-wrapped C191; maker0013 separately offers270mm/184g
Carbon Road. C222 is a third named, filament-wound design in350/390mm lengths.
Guida1996 explicitly saysC191 and270mm/180–190g; its heading is not a
transcription error and must not be relabelled Carbon Road merely from length.
Guida1999 explicitly saysC191,350mm/200g and alloy core. Preserve these claims
in11613, and represent the separately named1997 Road and C222 offerings
separately. Maker0010 includes26.6mm in addition to26.8/27/27.2mm.

**KTI requires a generation link.** Guida1996 printed154 gives Titec KTI,
nylon shell, titanium rails, black Kevlar cover and198g, with no photograph
or Berserkr name. The1997 maker's0011 says its Berserkr saddles have a new
profile; the dealer sheet lists Berserkr KTI at257g. Shared KTI/material
wording does not establish identical shell/profile or prove either weight
wrong. Keep11679 unchanged and hold the base Berserkr KTI insertion to avoid
a potential duplicate. The other five named Berserkr configurations differ
in rail/cover or explicitly named downhill profile.

**Titanium post is announcement only.** Maker0010 announces new titanium
posts coming mid1997 but supplies no dimensions, weight or product code.
That does not identify1999 titanium11858 or attest its precise configuration.
Keep11858 unchanged; no speculative earlier row or date move.
