## Sugino Cycle Parts catalogue (8 pages, 1978, Reminiscence Part 30) — data_source id 210

- `data_source` 210, label "Sugino Cycle Parts catalogue (1978)". Source:
  http://cyclespeugeot.web.fc2.com/reminiscence/sugino78.html, JPGs
  `catalog/sugino78/sugino78_01-08.jpg` (640 x ~670 px; no larger version).
  Japanese, Sugino Tekkosho (Osaka head office, Tokyo service station), "Sugino
  Cycle Parts". Dated 1978 by the site; page 3 carries a news flash about the
  Aug 1977 World Championships in Venezuela (Nakano and Sugano, 1st and 2nd,
  on the Mighty series), so it was printed after that. Pages: 1 cover, 2 "Super
  Star" hero crank, 3 news flash, 4 Super Mighty (MCVS, MCVD, MCC, MCS, MCD) and
  the BB system (shafts MS-68, MW-68 / 70, MT-68 / 70; hangers Super Deluxe,
  Deluxe, H, L), 5 Super Maxy (MCX-2l, NJX-2d, DJX-2, NJX-1, DJX-1) and BB sets, 6
  Idol (NJi-I, NFi-I, DJi-I, DFi-I, SFi) and BB shaft dimensions, 7 special
  cranks (MT Mighty Tour, Pro-D Prodynamic, Maxy Junior Sports, FC cycle
  soccer, MCS Demi-fond, MCS Cross-country), 8 Mighty seat post and parts
  (seat pin, bottle, titanium BB set, tools). The site says Mighty Competition
  became Super Mighty (track PCD 151 -> 144 mm, like the road crank), Maxy became
  Super Maxy, Pro-dai became Pro-dynamic, and the seat post was added.
- Updated (all `source_ref` -> 210, all velobase before): cranksets 1963 Super
  Mighty Competition and 1962 Super Mighty Track `year_from` 1980 -> 1978,
  1948 / 1949 Mighty Tour double / triple 1980 -> 1978; undated -> 1978-1978:
  1961 Super Maxy, 1960 Super Maxy (144BCD) (MCX-2l), 1976 Super Maxy "fix
  chainring" (110BCD) (NJX-1 / DJX-1, outer ring fixed), 1940 Idol, 1955 Pro
  Dynamic; chainrings 1334 Mighty / Super Mighty Competition Track `year_from`
  1980 -> 1978, 1328 Pro-D (undated -> 1978); seat posts 5924 Mighty and 5925
  Mighty (fluted) `year_from` 1984 -> 1978 (SP-1N / SP-2N 235 / 247 g and SP-1C /
  SP-2C 228 / 238 g against the velobase 241 / 233 g averages); BBs 163 MS-68 and
  162 Super Mighty Pista (undated -> 1978), 161 Mighty MW-68 1980 -> 1978.
  **Judgements:** 1976 as NJX-1 / DJX-1 by the "fix chainring" wording, 1960 as
  MCX-2l by the 144 BCD, 162 as the Super Mighty track BB.
- New (7 rows, 1978-1978, group NULL): 8883 "MCVS / MCVD, Super Mighty Victory",
  8884 "Maxy Junior Sports" (42-44T, cranks 125 / 152), 8885 "FC" (cycle
  soccer, 24T), 8886 "MCS Demi-fond" (53-120T), 8887 "MCS Cross-country"
  (48-52T), 8888 "MW-70 / MT-68 / MT-70, Super Mighty BB sets", 8889 "Super
  Maxy BB sets (68-W / 70-W / 68-T / 70-T)".
- Left alone: Maxy rows 1943-1945 (1970), Mighty Competition 1920 (1972), Super
  Maxy "loose chainring" 1977 (1970-80, years already cover 1978), the "75"
  group (1980), Cycloid, GX / VP / VT / AC / AP lines, BMX cranks, Guines, ALP,
  PD-1000 pedals, 3129 Mighty headset. The individual MCS / MCD / MCC and NJX /
  DJX / NJi codes are in descriptions, not rows. Out of scope: seat pin, bottle,
  chainguards and cases, titanium BB set, tools.
- Deleted: none. Bike links on 1963 (1 spec), 5928, 1969, 1934 and 1957 are
  unaffected (years only moved earlier).
- Unresolved: eight unlinked Sugino bike specs ("Sugino DGT" 1984 x3, "Forged
  Sugino LP" 1985, "Sugino Corsa 42/52" and "Sugino-Bianchi Corsa" 1981,
  "Sugino Mighty 46/36/26T" and "XE300" 1993) are not in this catalogue; the
  1973, 1982, 1965 and 1995 pages are below.

## Sugino chain wheel catalogue (7 spreads, 1973, Reminiscence Part 29) — data_source id 211

- `data_source` 211, label "Sugino chain wheel catalogue (1973)". Source:
  http://cyclespeugeot.web.fc2.com/reminiscence/sugino73.html, JPGs
  `catalog/sugino73/sugino73_01-07.jpg` (720 x ~480). Japanese, Sugino Tekkosho
  Osaka, "since 1910"; crank-only; sizes compatible with Campagnolo (site). The
  earliest Sugino source. Pages: 2 Mighty Competition (MCS single, MCD double, MCC
  custom and black, MCT triple; cranks 165 / 168 / 171 / 175 mm, single 40-54T,
  double 42-54T, 55-62T on order), 3 Mighty Tour (inner 34 / 36 / 40 / 42 / 44 /
  45T, outer 48 / 50 / 52T, cranks 165 / 171) and Dynamic Professional (outer
  48-52T, inner 32-50T, 28T on order), 4 Maxy (SJX single, DJX / DFX double, TJX
  triple; type 1 inner swap, type 2 inner and outer), 5 new products (MCV /
  MCV Black Mighty Victory 46-54T, New Maxy NJX-1, NJX-2, NJX-2AL, titanium MW-68
  shaft set and hanger sets), 6-7 tools, cotterless shaft sets, SDX / DX / H
  hangers and the shaft dimension table (MS-68, MW-68 / 70, MT-68 / 70, PW-68 /
  70, PT-68 / 70).
- Updated (all `source_ref` -> 211): 1920 Mighty Competition `year_to` 1972 -> 1973;
  1326 chainring, 1947 Mighty Comp (pista) and 1946 Mighty (road) (undated ->
  1973; 1946 is **a judgement**, the drilled MCC custom); 1943 Maxy 5-Arm
  `year_to` 1970 -> 1973; 1948 / 1949 Mighty Tour, 1955 Pro Dynamic and 1328 Pro-D
  `year_from` 1978 -> 1973 (1955 and 1328 are "Dynamic Professional" here, the 1978
  entry says formerly "Pro-dai"); 161 MW-68 and 163 MS-68 `year_from` 1978 -> 1973.
- New (3 rows, 1973-1973): 8890 "MCV / MCV Black, Mighty Victory", 8891 "NJX-1 /
  NJX-2 / NJX-2AL, New Maxy", 8892 "PW-68 / PW-70 / PT-68 / PT-70, Pro-D BB shafts".
- Left alone: the 3-bolt Maxy rows 1944 / 1945 (1970, not in this catalogue),
  the tools, the cotterless shaft sets (descriptions only) and the SDX / DX / H
  hangers. Naming history: 1973 Mighty Competition -> 1978 Super Mighty; Maxy / New
  Maxy -> Super Maxy; Dynamic Professional -> Pro-Dynamic.

## Sugino Techno catalogue Vol. 55 (3 images, 1982, Reminiscence Part 48) — data_source id 212

- `data_source` 212, label "Sugino Techno catalogue Vol. 55 (1982)". Source:
  http://cyclespeugeot.web.fc2.com/reminiscence/sugino82.html, JPGs
  `catalog/sugino82/sugino82_01-03.jpg` (360 / 720 / 360 px; **the page 3 spec
  table is unreadable**, so only models and dates were used, no weights or tooth
  ranges). English and Japanese; the company became Sugino Techno in 1982 and the
  cover is the GUINES set (site: sold in 1,000 sets to 72 countries, serial-numbered,

  10-speed (AT TAT, PX TPX, GT TGT, No. 6 FE-RB, No. 5 FE-WB), BMX chainwheels (CT-BMX,
  AT-BMX, GT-BMX, Maxy Cross, OPC-SA-BJ, OPC-XJ) and BMX stems (HP-UFO, HP-G2, HP-G3,
  HP-G3A, HP-G4).
- Updated (all `source_ref` -> 212): `year_to` -> 1982 on 1925 Aero Mighty, 1926 Aero
  Mighty Techno (**a judgement**, the catalogue shows only the two Aero Mighty),
  1932 AS, 1934 GS, 1964 / 1965 AT (duplicates of one model), 1937 GT, 1967 GT
  (triple), 1953 No.6 FE-RB; undated -> 1982: 1957 PX, 1931 CT-BMX. Guines 160 /
  1938 already 1982, unchanged.
- New (7 rows, 1982-1982): 8893 AT-BMX, 8894 GT-BMX, 8895 Maxy Cross, 8896 OPC-SA / BJ,
  8897 OPC-XJ, 8898 No.5 FE-WB, 8899 "HP-UFO / HP-G2 / HP-G3 / HP-G3A / HP-G4, BMX stems".
- Left alone: HP-GL stem (6690), BMX 81 (1930), Carbon BMX (1327). Bike links on 1934
  (2 specs) and 1957 (1) are unaffected (years only extend).

## Sugino chain wheel leaflet (c. 1965, Reminiscence Part 63) — data_source id 213

- `data_source` 213, label "Sugino chain wheel leaflet (c. 1965)". Source:
  http://cyclespeugeot.web.fc2.com/reminiscence/sugino65.html, JPGs
  `catalog/sugino65/1965sugino_01, 03-09.jpg` (720 px wide, 293-876 px high; **image
  02 is linked nowhere and 404s**, so the inside left page is missing; 09 is a
  photo of a cotterless Mighty 5 with a Campagnolo "68-SS-120" shaft, not a
  catalogue page). Japanese, Sugino Tekkosho, Osaka, "JASCA group" (name changed to
  Sugino Techno in 1982). Dated c. 1965 by the site from New Cycling ads: the
  Pro-dynamic was a cottered crank in Aug 1965 and cotterless in Oct 1965, and the
  leaflet shows the cotterless crank. Pages: 1 front and back cover, 3 spread of
  all models, 4 Pro-dynamic, 5 High-Double I (DJ-1 / DF-1) and II (DJ-2A / DJ-2B),
  6 High-Triple I / II (TJ-1 / TJ-2A / TJ-2B) and Howard (HJ, PAT. 75509, 4 rings),
  7 Mighty 5 (PAT. 436235) and Dynamic (hollow Ni-Cr steel, cranks 165 / 168 / 171),
  8 imports: Atom hub (410 g), Normandy hub (508 g), Normandy Sport hub (590 g), Atom
  Sport hub (510 g), Huret wing nuts (Luxe, Vit), TA bottle with cage. Sugino made
  a technical tie-up with Maillard and began knock-down production in June 1965.
- Updated (all `source_ref` -> 213): 1954 Pro Dynamic (Cottered) `year_to` 1960 ->
  1965 (**a judgement**: advertised Aug 1965); 1955 Pro Dynamic and 1328 Pro-D
  `year_from` 1973 -> 1965; 3600 Normandy hub `year_from` 1970 -> 1965 (Sugino-
  badged Maillard Normandy, 508 g, 36 holes).
- New (7 rows, 1965-1965): 8900 "High-Double I / II (DJ-1, DF-1, DJ-2A, DJ-2B)", 8901
  "High-Triple I / II, TJ-1 / TJ-2A / TJ-2B" (48-45-36T), 8902 "Howard, HJ (4-ring)"
  (51-48-45-36T), 8903 "Mighty 5" (cottered and cotterless, cranks 165 / 168 / 171),
  8904 "Dynamic (Dyna-hollow)", 8905 "Atom hub / Atom Sport hub", 8906 "Normandy
  Sport hub".
- Left alone: 1952 No. 5 / Silstar (1970, not in this leaflet), 1944 / 1945 Maxy
  3-bolt, 1920 Mighty Competition (starts 1972; the site calls the Mighty 5 its
  ancestor, no year change), the wing nuts and the TA bottle (no category).
- Deleted: none. No bike links or overrides touch the rows changed here.
