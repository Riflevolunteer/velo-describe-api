# Selle Italia catalogs processed so far

## R. J. Chicken & Sons trade catalogue, Selle Italia section — 7 November 1979 (Saddles_from_R_J_Chicken_catalogue_1979-11-07.pdf, 8 pp, scan, no text layer), data_source id 176

- Source: https://www.icenicam.org.uk/library/Selle_Italia/Saddles_from_R_J_Chicken_catalogue_1979-11-07.pdf
  (IceniCAM). An excerpt of the UK distributor R. J. Chicken & Sons Ltd's
  trade catalogue (tel. Watford 33027), the same 7 Nov 1979 catalogue as the
  Maillard section (data_source 104, see `maillard.md`). PDF pp.: 1 catalogue
  index (Sella Italia = cat. pp. 35-38), 2 blank, 3-6 = cat. pp. 35-38, 7 =
  the pink trade price sheet (cat. p. 68: Sedis chain, Sella Italia, Steco,
  Vredestein, Weinmann), 8 IceniCAM cover. Pages spell it "Sella Italia";
  the logo says "Selle Italia".
- Dated by filename (7 Nov 1979). A distributor's catalogue is a UK
  first-appearance record, not a start date: models may predate it abroad.
  "NEW" flags on the range intro, Condor, Anatomic, Lady and Criterium;
  Superleggera "Introduction late 1978".
- First Selle Italia source: all 57 prior rows (brand 570; 52 saddles, 5 seat
  posts) were bare velobase. **No part numbers**, models by name only; the
  price sheet gives pack size and trade price in decimal pounds
  (e.g. Grand Prix 0.95, T.I. 1.50, Sprint 3.07, Condor 4.50, Anatomic 5.55,
  Lady 5.91, Criterium 7.50, Superleggera 11.00; Condor seat pin 3.25,
  Competition seat pin 5.30).
