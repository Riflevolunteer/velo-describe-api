# MAFAC catalogs processed so far

## MAFAC catalogue, brakes and accessories — c. 1976 (ebykr.com, 21 webp scans)

- `data_source` 143. Source:
  https://ebykr.com/library/mafac-1976-catalog-brakes-accessories/
  (ebykr-1976-mafac-catalog-p01..p21.webp, Eric Anschutz collection,
  "1976 (estimated)"). Bilingual French/English, Manufacture Arvernoise de
  Freins et Accessoires pour Cycles, Clermont-Ferrand; no printed date. The
  postcode 63100 and SIREN number put it after 1973, the anodized "2000"
  and the "new translucent hood" insert fit the mid-70s, so 1976 adopted.
  Printed pages 2-5, 8-19 plus an unnumbered insert, components table and
  covers are present; **printed pp. 6-7 (the Racer brake page) are not in
  the scan**. Racer is known here only from the components and dimension
  tables (R1 / R1 P / R2 GL / R2 GLP / R3 L / R3 LP).
- Listed: brakes Compétition Cn 1/2/3 (+ A silver / D gold anodized), 2000
  M 1-3 A/D, Racer RGL/RL, Raid Rd GL/L, "S" Sb L/SL, Critérium KGL/KL,
  Jacky réf. J, Cyclo-Tandem HC 4/5 and HZ 4/5; levers racing "dural"
  CB/CS (PM short, hoods RMT/RMGT, cover F), racing "2000" C/CA/CD RMEGT,
  Ville VF B/S, Guidonnet GF B/S, Ville VDA B/S, Guidonnet GDA B/S,
  children's VE/GE/CE/CET, inverted I/19.3 / I/20.8 / I/19.8. Catalogue
  uses letter codes and exploded-drawing part numbers (arms 1454G/D,
  3454G/D, 2454G/D, 4454G/D, 54G/D, 254G/D, 654G/D; stirrups 451/452/1451;
  pivots 491E/493E/478E, 51E/52E/251E/252E); the velobase lever numbers
  419/429, 519/529, 218, 130, 121 and "Forge 820" appear nowhere in it.
  Out of scope: cable hangers 65 / 65 P / 499 / 499 c / 499 P / 499 cP (+ Z),
  pulley 75-78 à 80, clamp 77 à 80, pivots T1/T2/450a, pads 64/664/264,
  cables, tool kits 45 and 48.
