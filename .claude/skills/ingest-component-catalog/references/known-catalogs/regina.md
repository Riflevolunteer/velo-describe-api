# Regina catalogs processed so far

## Regina Extra catalogue C/7 109/E, English edition (c. 1970) — retrobike.co.uk archive 1184, data_source id 117

- Source: https://www.retrobike.co.uk/archive/1970-regina-catalogue.1184/download
  (`r.pdf`, img2pdf, 12 pp; the download 406s without a Referer from the
  archive page). S.I.C.C. Regina S.p.A., Cinisello Balsamo; "Roller chains
  and freewheels for bicycles and mopeds". **No printed date** — "1970" is
  the archive's title; this copy carries a Specialized Bicycle Imports
  (Campbell CA) stamp, and Specialized dates from 1974, so it was being
  handed out 1974+. Only 1970 was used for years (NULL -> 1970-1970;
  existing ranges left).
- First Regina source: all 80 prior rows were bare velobase. The catalogue
  identifies parts by **number** (50, 50 oro, 51, 53, 54 C, 80/81;
  freewheel codes 00902099 / 00912099 / 0090xxxxxx), not the model names
  velobase uses — no "Record", "America", "Corsa" anywhere. «Oro quality»
  is stated to be the same dimensions with selected parts and finer finish.
  Chain 50 = «Supercorsa» (race service case: 6 chains «Supercorsa» type
  50 x 116; lid box "Regina Gran Sport Super Corsa"). 6-speed is the top
  multiple; 5-speed «scalare» 13-17 on its own 22.7 mm body.
- Retitled (old title kept for matching): 1376 "Regina Extra Gran Sport
  Super Corsa" -> "Regina Extra 50, Gran Sport Super Corsa" (1970-1970).
- Updated (source_ref -> 117): 1382 Extra 50 Oro (desc; 1970-1980 kept);
  2170 Extra (1 speed) (00902099 / 00912099 detail, NULL -> 1970); 2174
  Extra (5 speed) and 2175 Extra (6 speed) (combinations, weights, 2-notch
  body; 2175 NULL -> 1970); 2182 Extra Oro (5 speed) (NULL -> 1970); 2194
  Oro (6 speed) (desc only; 1970-1980 kept, links/overrides unaffected).
- Inserted (1970-1970, UUID source_id): chains 8166 Extra 51, 8167 Extra
  53, 8168 Extra 54 C, 8169 Extra 80 / 81 (block chain); freewheels 8170
  Extra (4 speed), 8171 Extra (5 speed, scalare).
- Deleted: 1379 "Regina Gran Sport Super Corsa" (Chains, undated, velobase,
  `EC63B0D7-29B3-47D2-8BFD-BA982A5FD25A`) — duplicate of 1376. No links /
  overrides.
- Not touched: 3-speeds 2171/2172/2173 (split by remover type, dated
  1950-52; catalogue says standard bodies have 2 notches, splined on
  request — unclear whether 2173 "wide 2 prong" is the 1970 part); 1381
  "50 Record ORO" and 1383 "Record Super Star" (no "Record" in the
  catalogue; 1381 probably = 1382); 1377 "Extra Super Racing" (catalogue's
  «super racing» = the oro racing chain type, too indirect to merge);
  moped chains (54, 90 R, ASA 41/35, 120-122) and moped freewheels
  0090-0092 XY99 out of scope.
- Candidate for a later pass: 2177 / 2178 are both "Regina Extra (7 speed,
  silver)" 1980 — duplicate pair, not covered by this catalogue.
- Bike link candidate: 1973 Raleigh "1/2" x 3/32" Regina ORO" chain — the
  catalogue makes 1/2 x 3/32 oro the 50 oro, i.e. 1382.

## Regina Extra Catalogo C-78 (1978) — velo-pages.com album via Wayback, data_source id 138

- Source: velo-pages.com Gallery2 album `g2_itemId=5934` ("Regina catalog
  (1978)", folder 90 "Regina"), read through the Wayback Machine. 16 pages:
  page 1 = item 5935, pages 2-16 = 5940 + 3(N-2); display image = item+2 at
  `g2_serialNumber=2` (453x640). Archived: cover, printed pp. 5, 6, 7, 8, 10,
  11 and back cover; pp. 2-4, 9, 12-15 never were. Page 08's own item (5958,
  serial 2) is the 1237x1749 original, the only full-res scan archived.
  Cover "CATALOGO C-78" dates it. Back cover: Società Italiana Catene
  Calibrate Regina S.p.A., Viale Fulvio Testi 186, Cinisello Balsamo. IT/EN.
- vs c. 1970 (id 117): chains now have names: 50 Corsa (standard; the 1970
  "Supercorsa"), 50 Oro ("BC" riveted bushes, gold outer plates), and new
  50 Record (drilled outer plates, gold), 50 Record S (nickel-plated), 50
  Titanio (titanium plates). Freewheels: the standard multiples are the
  "Corsa" type; new Tripla Sport (narrow 16-18-20, only model with the
  splined hub, 28 mm spanner), 6-speed scalare on the 22.7 mm body, Oro
  4/5/6, Titanio (steel body, Ti sprockets) and Titanio Superstar (all Ti
  except balls/pawls/springs). Corsa weights are a little lower than 1970's
  (5-speed 309-393 g vs 316-400 g); carried as dated text.
- Retitled (old title kept for matching), source_ref -> 138:
  1376 "Regina Extra 50, Gran Sport Super Corsa" -> "Regina Extra 50 Corsa"
  (year_to 1970 -> 1978); 1381 'Regina 50 "Record" ORO' -> "Regina Extra 50
  Record" (1970-1980 kept, 390 g kept); 1391 "Regina Titanio" (Chains) ->
  "Regina Extra 50 Titanio" (year_to -> 1978, 250 g Actual kept);
  2197 "Regina Sport (3 speed)" -> "Regina Extra Tripla Sport (3 speed)"
  (NULL -> 1978-1978); 2167 "Regina Corsa, Italy 81 (3 speed)" -> "Regina
  Extra Corsa (3 speed)" (NULL -> 1978-1978; "Italy 81" read as a body
  stamp, kept in the description); 8170 "Regina Extra (4 speed)" -> "Regina
  Extra Corsa (4 speed)", 2174 "Regina Extra (5 speed)" -> "Regina Extra
  Corsa (5 speed)", 2175 "Regina Extra (6 speed)" -> "Regina Extra Corsa (6
  speed)" (all year_to -> 1978); 2202 "Regina Titanio (5 speed)" -> "Regina
  Extra Titanio (5 / 6 speed)" (year_to -> 1978, 200 g Actual kept).
- Updated, title kept, source_ref -> 138: 1382 Extra 50 Oro (desc: BC
  bushes, gold plates; 4 bike links unaffected); 2170 Extra (1 speed)
  (year_to -> 1978); 8171 Extra (5 speed, scalare) (13-14-15-17-19 option,
  year_to -> 1978); 2182 Extra Oro (5 speed) (gold sprockets, year_to ->
  1978); 2194 Oro (6 speed) (gold sprockets; 5 links unaffected).
- Inserted (1978-1978, UUID source_id): Chains 8334 Extra 50 Record S;
  Freewheels 8335 Extra (6 speed, scalare), 8336 Extra Oro (4 speed), 8337
  Extra Titanio Superstar (5 / 6 speed).
- Deleted (merged; no bike_spec links, no overrides):
  2165 "Regina Corsa, Italy 78 (4 speed)" (Freewheels, undated, velobase,
  `D9010195-5B42-4CA2-8410-4883BAAC1409`) -> 8170;
  2162 "Regina Corsa (5 speed)" (Freewheels, 1970, velobase,
  `6C3F341D-ED8B-43B4-ACB3-1353EA4787E9`, 355 g Actual moved into 2174) ->
  2174; 2166 "Regina Corsa, Italy 80 (5 speed)" (Freewheels, undated,
  velobase, `61FCD18D-D17D-4F8C-AB96-C4CB575EE664`) -> 2174.
- Generator check: re-running the 7 Regina-bearing bike CSVs resolves every
  Regina spec to the same rows as before; no override changes needed.
- Not touched / flagged: 1385 "Extra 'Oro' (center drilled)" (415 g; only
  the 50 Record is drilled in 1978, so possibly the Record); 1375 "Extra
  (center drilled)"; 1383 "Record Super Star" (Chains; the catalogue has a
  Titanio Superstar *freewheel*, no Superstar chain); 2183 "Extra Record
  (Ti) (5,6 speed)" (221 g avg, possibly the Titanio Superstar); 2202's
  200 g is light for a steel-body Titanio, may really be a Superstar; 2163 /
  2164 Corsa 6-speed gold / silver; 2172 "(3 speed, splined remover)";
  2177 / 2178 7-speed duplicate pair still open.
- Out of scope: chain spares n. 4 / 7 / 26 / 27 / 30, rivet extractor type
  1, cutting/rejoining pliers, Regina Cycle Lube (165 cc), single-sprocket
  weight table (p. 11).

## Regina Extra: VeloBase archive PDFs — Regina USA catalogue (1992), plus two 1970s files — data_source id 190

- Source: VeloBase's own catalogue-scan archive
  (velobase.com/Pages/Resources/CatalogScans.aspx, files fetched with
  `DownloadFile.aspx?Path=/CatalogScans/<name>&FileName=<name>`), three PDFs, no
  text layer:
  - `ReginaExtra_1970s_2.pdf` (12 pp): "Catalog C/7 109/E", English edition,
    S.I.C.C. Regina S.p.A., Cinisello Balsamo, roller chains and freewheels for
    bicycles and mopeds. **A second copy of data_source 117** (same catalogue
    code); not re-ingested.
  - `ReginaExtra_1970s.pdf` (4 pp): undated bilingual Italian / English leaflet
    (Milan, Via A. Manuzio 15): chains 50 / 51 / 53 / 54 and block chains 80 /
    81, spare links 4 / 7 / 12 / 26 / 27 / 30, single freewheels 16-24T and 3 to
    6 speed combinations (2.10mm tooth, 19 to 30mm wide). Same numbers as the
    rows already held from 117 (8166-8169, 1376); nothing changed.
  - `1992ReginaExtra.pdf` (8 pp): **Regina USA, Inc., 311 Commerce Drive,
    Easton, Maryland, 1992** ("more than 70 years of quality"), recorded as
    data_source 190 "Regina USA catalogue (1992)". Pages: chains (Hollow Pin,
    Anniversario, CX-S, 50 America S.L.), "new and improved" freewheels (Synchro
    1992, America 1992, America Superlight), the chain table, spokes and nipples,
    the Enduro mountain-bike page and the four group sets (America, Strada,
    Stelvio, Enduro) with weights.
- 1992 chain table (overall width mm / kg per metre; the "W" column printed 3.30
  for every chain at scan size and was not used): 53 Turismo 9.3 / 0.29, 51 Sport
  9.1 / 0.28, 51 CX-Pista 9.1 / 0.28, 50 Corsa 8.1 / 0.26, 50 Oro 8.1 / 0.26, 50
  Silver 8.1 / 0.26, 50 Racing 7.2 / 0.23, 50 Super Racing 7.2 / 0.23, 50 S.L.
  7.2 / 0.19, 50 America S.L. 7.2 / 0.19, 50 CX-S 7.2 / 0.23, 50 Anniversario
  7.2 / 0.23. The 1970 51 was 9.3mm / 0.29, so the 51 Sport's spec differs between
  editions and 8166 names both.
- Group sets (1992): rear derailleur America 230g (28T), Strada 285g (28T, cable
  saver, spoke-barrier cage), Stelvio 295g (32T), Enduro 360g (40T capacity, 32T
  maximum); front America 100g with clip (90g braze-on), Strada 130g, Stelvio and
  Enduro 150g; shifters 90g (Stelvio frame 90g / handlebar 215g, Enduro 200g);
  chainwheel and crank 710g (America, Strada) and 770g (Stelvio, Enduro); bottom
  bracket 340 / 340 / 350 / 350g; freewheel America 6-speed 13-21T 360g and 7-speed
  415g, Strada Synchro 90 6-speed 350g and 7-speed 410g, Stelvio and Enduro
  14-32T about 500 / 510g.
- Updated, source_ref -> 190: chains 1376 50 Corsa and 1382 50 Oro (year_to ->
  1992 only), 1388 Anniversario, 1384 CX / CX-S, 1389 America Superleggera (the 50
  America S.L.), 1390 Superleggera SL (50 S.L.), 1387 Racing (50 Racing), 1377 Super
  Racing (years none -> 1992), 8166 Extra 51 (51 Sport) and 8167 Extra 53 (53
  Turismo); freewheels 2200 Synchro 92, 2158 / 2159 America S 1992, 2203 America
  1992 Superleggera (the America Superlight, years left NULL), 2205 Synchro 90-S and
  2207 Synchro 90 (year_to -> 1992); group parts 1716 America crank, 1717 Strada
  crank, 4378 / 4377 / 4376 America / Strada / Enduro rear derailleurs, 2422 / 2423
  America / Strada front derailleurs, 6098 America shifters. Measured weights kept.
- Inserted (1992-1992, UUID source_id, group_id NULL): 8798 Regina Extra 51
  CX-Pista and 8799 Regina Extra 50 Silver (Chains). "Silver BX" 1386 is a
  different name and was left alone.
- Not inserted: the Stelvio group parts, the Strada shifters, the Enduro crank,
  front derailleur and shifters, Regina bottom brackets (there are no Regina BB
  rows) and the Hollow Pin chain (no specification given).
- Does not resolve the unlinked bike specs "Regina C Record" chain (5 bikes;
  the Campagnolo-branded C-Record chain made by Regina, so a Campagnolo catalogue
  is the place to look) and "Regina Extra Super Corsa 1/2 x 3/32" (3 bikes; it
  may be 1376, "Gran Sport Super Corsa" in c. 1970, but that is an override
  choice, not a catalogue finding).
- Out of scope: spokes and nipples, chain spare links, rivet extractors and
  freewheel removers, the race service case, speed ratio tables.
