# Regina catalogs processed so far

## Regina Extra catalogue C/7 109/E, English edition (c. 1970) — retrobike.co.uk archive 1184, data_source id 117

- Source: https://www.retrobike.co.uk/archive/1970-regina-catalogue.1184/download
  (`r.pdf`, img2pdf, 12 pp; the download 406s without a Referer from the
  archive page). S.I.C.C. Regina S.p.A., Cinisello Balsamo; "Roller chains
  and freewheels for bicycles and mopeds". **No printed date** — "1970" is
  the archive's title; this copy carries a Specialized Bicycle Imports
  (Campbell CA) stamp, and Specialized dates from 1974, so it was being
  handed out 1974+. Only 1970 was used for years (NULL -> 1970-1970;
  existing ranges left).
- First Regina source: all 80 prior rows were bare velobase. The catalogue
  identifies parts by **number** (50, 50 oro, 51, 53, 54 C, 80/81;
  freewheel codes 00902099 / 00912099 / 0090xxxxxx), not the model names
  velobase uses — no "Record", "America", "Corsa" anywhere. «Oro quality»
  is stated to be the same dimensions with selected parts and finer finish.
  Chain 50 = «Supercorsa» (race service case: 6 chains «Supercorsa» type
  50 x 116; lid box "Regina Gran Sport Super Corsa"). 6-speed is the top
  multiple; 5-speed «scalare» 13-17 on its own 22.7 mm body.
- Retitled (old title kept for matching): 1376 "Regina Extra Gran Sport
  Super Corsa" -> "Regina Extra 50, Gran Sport Super Corsa" (1970-1970).
- Updated (source_ref -> 117): 1382 Extra 50 Oro (desc; 1970-1980 kept);
  2170 Extra (1 speed) (00902099 / 00912099 detail, NULL -> 1970); 2174
  Extra (5 speed) and 2175 Extra (6 speed) (combinations, weights, 2-notch
  body; 2175 NULL -> 1970); 2182 Extra Oro (5 speed) (NULL -> 1970); 2194
  Oro (6 speed) (desc only; 1970-1980 kept, links/overrides unaffected).
- Inserted (1970-1970, UUID source_id): chains 8166 Extra 51, 8167 Extra
  53, 8168 Extra 54 C, 8169 Extra 80 / 81 (block chain); freewheels 8170
  Extra (4 speed), 8171 Extra (5 speed, scalare).
- Deleted: 1379 "Regina Gran Sport Super Corsa" (Chains, undated, velobase,
  `EC63B0D7-29B3-47D2-8BFD-BA982A5FD25A`) — duplicate of 1376. No links /
  overrides.
- Not touched: 3-speeds 2171/2172/2173 (split by remover type, dated
  1950-52; catalogue says standard bodies have 2 notches, splined on
  request — unclear whether 2173 "wide 2 prong" is the 1970 part); 1381
  "50 Record ORO" and 1383 "Record Super Star" (no "Record" in the
  catalogue; 1381 probably = 1382); 1377 "Extra Super Racing" (catalogue's
  «super racing» = the oro racing chain type, too indirect to merge);
  moped chains (54, 90 R, ASA 41/35, 120-122) and moped freewheels
  0090-0092 XY99 out of scope.
- Candidate for a later pass: 2177 / 2178 are both "Regina Extra (7 speed,
  silver)" 1980 — duplicate pair, not covered by this catalogue.
- Bike link candidate: 1973 Raleigh "1/2" x 3/32" Regina ORO" chain — the
  catalogue makes 1/2 x 3/32 oro the 50 oro, i.e. 1382.
