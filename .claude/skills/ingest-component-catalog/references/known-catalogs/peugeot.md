# Peugeot catalogs processed so far

## Peugeot "Cycles Peugeot Parts List" (Honda Motor, Japan) — 1982 (bikeboompeugeot.com, 31 scans) — light ingest

- `data_source` 97, label "Peugeot Parts List, Japan/Honda (1982)". Source:
  bikeboompeugeot.com "Parts and Accessories/Peugeot 1982 Japan Bicycle
  Parts List Catalog" Pg1..31.jpg (linked from the 1982 Japan Brochure
  page). Honda-distributed service parts book for the seven Japan-market
  models PX10DU.J, PF10J, PH8SJ-GC, PH8SJ-GT, PH65J, C50CXJ, C46MDL.J:
  exploded diagrams pp. 4-17, then part-number / yen-price / fitment table
  pp. 18-31. Japanese only. Peugeot/Honda part numbers (09xP6-xxxxxx);
  maker named in brackets only for some lines.
- Why only a light ingest: the headsets (the Peugeot 1979 bike-spec gap)
  are listed as Peugeot part numbers (095P6-114882 PX10, 095P5-106987
  PF10, 095P0-090806 others) with no maker, so they do not identify the
  1979 "Peugeot, non-loosening" / Lightrace headsets. Derailleurs are all
  Simplex and all already in the DB with compatible years (SLJ A522 front
  on PX10; SJA222, SXA22 front; SLJ5500 CP, S061 TSP, SX610 TSP, SX410 T,
  SX100 T, ST0P rear). Rims Mavic 700C, chain Sedisport, tyres
  Hutchinson / Wolber / Michelin, Shimano 3-speed hub on PH65J — generic,
  nothing to add.
- New (2 rows, 1982, p. 21 items 32/33): 7947 "Maillard E-100, front hub"
  (094P6-108823 PX10 "Maillard-made" ¥9,000; 094P5-116832 PF10 ¥2,800),
  7948 "Maillard E-126, rear hub" (094P6-116655 PX10 "Maillard-made"
  ¥12,000; PF10, PH8SJ, 28h on C50/C46). Maker annotation appears only on
  the PX10 lines; the cheaper same-code lines are assumed the same model.
- Not added: E-96 front hub (094P2-017581 36h, 094P0-114318 28h, PH/C
  models) — no maker given, so not attributed. Maillard quick-release
  units items 43/44 (094P5-103943 / 103952) out of scope. Pedals, cranks
  (BSA-threaded, no maker), brakes, saddles, bars/stems: no maker named.
- Bike links unchanged (no 1982 Peugeot bikes in the DB). The earlier gap
  analysis called Normandy "absent" — wrong: Normandy hubs are in the DB
  under the Maillard brand (3383, 3384, 3388-3391).
