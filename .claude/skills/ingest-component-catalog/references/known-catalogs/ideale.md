# Ideale (Thron & Bethet, France) — saddles

## Ron Kitching 1981 Ideale range page and an undated Anglo-Continental advert — Classic Rendezvous Ideale page (updated 10.22.2013), data_source 280 / 281

- Source: https://classicrendezvous.com/country-of-origin/france/french-parts/ideale/
  — a photo gallery. Two dated documents on it: `Kitching_range_81.gif` (Ron
  Kitching 1981 catalogue page, "entire range of Ideale saddles", sizes and
  weights; data_source 280, GB) and `Ideal64adL.jpg` (Anglo-Continental Cycles,
  Harrogate, "3 New Introductions from Ideale"; captioned "1940s Cycling
  magazine" but the file name says 64 and prices are pre-decimal, so treated as
  undated; data_source 281, GB). Captions add "mod. 90 introduced in 1965" and
  photo-only models 45, 52, 57, 59, 65, 72 Cyclo-Touriste, Columbia, a cut-away
  "swallow" and a 2004 "Super Comfort". Firm dated 1890 to the mid 1980s.
- First source for the brand: no Ideale saddle rows existed. New brand 697
  "Ideale" (no accent, so the "Ideale ..." bike specs can match; the matcher does
  not strip accents), category_brand Saddles. Brand 371 "Ideal" (a 1930s drum-brake
  hub) is a different maker and was left alone.
- New rows (14, ids 9773-9786, Saddles, no group): 2000 (1981), 2001 (1974-1981),
  2002 (1976-1981), 2003 (1981), 2004 Randonneuse (1981), No. 80 Record
  (1976-1981), No. 80 Speciale (1981), No. 88 Competition Speciale (1980-1981),
  No. 90 Competition Speciale (1965-1981), No. 90R Competition Speciale (1981),
  No. 92 Diagonale KR (1980-1981), all source_ref 280; No. 60 Record, No. 60
  Professionnel, No. 47 (years NULL, source_ref 281).
- Year evidence beyond 1981: year_from from dated bike specs already in the DB
  (Peugeot / Motobecane "IDEALE 2001" 1974, "Ideale 2002" 1976, "Ideale 80" 1976,
  "Ideale 88 / 92" options 1980) and the site caption for the 90 (1965; the
  earliest catalogue-backed attestation is the 1973 Peugeot "Brooks Professional
  or Ideale 90"). year_to 1981 is the last attestation, not an end date.
- Judgement: "Ideale 80" (2 bikes, 1976) is taken to be the 80 Record (the
  standard model), so the 80 Record starts 1976 and the 80 Speciale 1981.
- Left: gallery-only models (no specs, no dates); the Ideale saddle cover (no
