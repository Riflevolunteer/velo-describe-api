## R. J. Chicken & Sons trade catalogue, Laprade section — 7 November 1979 (Laprade/Seat_pillars_from_R_J_Chicken_catalogue_1979-11-07.pdf, 6 pp, scan, no text layer), data_source id 182

- Source: https://www.icenicam.org.uk/library/Laprade/Seat_pillars_from_R_J_Chicken_catalogue_1979-11-07.pdf
  (IceniCAM). The Laprade section (cat. pp. 9-10) of the UK distributor R. J.
  Chicken & Sons Ltd's trade catalogue; the same 7 Nov 1979 catalogue as
  Maillard (104), Selle Italia (176), Sedis (177), Vredestein (179), Weinmann
  (180) and Ofmega (181). PDF pp.: 1 index, 2 blank, 3 = cat. p. 9 (intro,
  the 1/4-turn fitting, Model 606), 4 = cat. p. 10 (Model 505, exploded
  view), 5 = the first page of the pink trade price list, headed "Suggested
  Trade Price List, November 7th, 1979" (the only place the exact date of
  the whole catalogue is printed), 6 IceniCAM cover.
- Laprade is described as a French manufacturer that "recently diversified"
  into bicycle components, so these may be among its first models. Common
  features: patented design with the fewest parts (pillar and cradle head,
  pivot support, securing plate, bolt and washer), fitted and adjusted from
  any angle with a single rear Allen-key bolt tightened 1/4 turn, strength
  tested to 90kg, quoted as "revolutionary" (Cycling) and "the most
  straight-forward seat pillar" (L'Officielle).
- Models: 606 (professional, forged lightweight alloy, bored tube,
  mechanically then hand polished, 200mm, 26.4 / 26.8 / 27.0 / 27.2mm,
  average 220g, fitted to the Carlton-Weinmann Professional Team bicycles;
  boxed with Allen key, trade 9.50) and 505 (forged lightweight alloy,
  mechanically polished pillar and cradle head, 200mm, 26.2 / 26.4 / 26.6 /
  26.8 / 27.0 / 27.2mm, 237-257g, fitted to the new Selle Italia "Lady"
  saddle; trade 6.00).
- Both models were already bare velobase rows (brand 597, source_ref 1,
  1970): 5816 Laprade Brevete 606 and 5815 Laprade Brevete 505. Updated,
  source_ref -> 182: year_to 1970 -> 1979 on both (the velobase "1970" looks
  like a decade bucket) and descriptions rewritten from the catalogue. The
  measured weights are kept as independent evidence: 606 "219 grams (avg)"
  agrees with the 220g average; 505 "310 grams (Actual)" is a single 27.2mm,
  24cm sample, longer than the catalogue's 200mm, so it is labelled as such.
  Titles unchanged ("Brevete" is the stamp on the posts), so no search_text
  change.
- No rows inserted or deleted. Left alone: 5812 / 5813 / 5814 Brevete
  [black anodized neck / fluted / long neck] (not in the catalogue), and the
  Sakae, Roto and Strong "Laprade style" copies (other brands).
- No bike_spec value names Laprade, so no links changed.
- Out of scope: the other companies' items on the price list page (Bierreci
  toe straps and clips, Esge kickstands, carriers and mudguards, Maillard
  hubs, pedals and freewheels; Maillard is data_source 104).
